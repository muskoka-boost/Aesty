The Stones

What goes into the pieces, and what each stone is traditionally said to carry.

  • Amazonite

    Pale blue-green, faintly cloudy. Associated with calm and with saying the thing you mean to say.

  • Sesame Jasper

    Cream flecked with black, no two beads alike. A grounding stone — the one that keeps the rest steady.

  • White Jade

    Soft and near-translucent. Linked to harmony and to letting go of the noise.

  • Blue Dot Jasper

    Deep blue scattered through pale stone. Balance, and a clear head.

About

Aesty Design started at a kitchen table in Orillia and has stayed about that size on purpose. Every piece is strung by hand, a few at a time, from stone chosen bead by bead rather than bought by the strand.

Nothing here is mass produced and nothing is rushed. If you want something in a particular colour, length or stone, that is usually the best way to work — tell us what you are after and we will put it together.
