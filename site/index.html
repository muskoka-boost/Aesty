About Aesty

Every piece passes through Angel's hands.

Aesty Design started in 2025 at a small table in Orillia. Angel picks each stone for its colour and feel, then strings it with gold-plated or sterling silver beads so it wears well for years, not weeks.

Right now that means earrings and bracelets. More is on the way. If you have something in mind that isn't here yet, ask. Custom work is half the fun.
